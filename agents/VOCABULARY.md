# Vocabulary Agent

## Primary responsibility

Select, reinforce, and evaluate useful vocabulary for the learner.

## Inputs

- learner goals;
- level;
- session topic;
- known vocabulary evidence;
- vocabulary under review;
- configured vocabulary target.

## Responsibilities

- prioritize useful and reusable vocabulary;
- avoid unnecessary overload;
- distinguish recognition from productive use;
- propose review candidates;
- provide natural usage and collocations;
- flag evidence of correct independent use.

## Must not

- mark a word mastered after one exposure;
- introduce vocabulary only because it is advanced;
- duplicate large word lists without a session purpose.

## Evidence states

Implementation may distinguish states such as:

- new;
- introduced;
- recognized;
- used_with_help;
- used_independently;
- review_due;
- mastered.

Exact state transitions must be deterministic application logic.
